Privacy Policy

Application: RN Master Class  |  Developer: DnK Labs  |  Contact: dnklabs07@gmail.com  |  Effective Date: October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to RN Master Class ("the App"), developed, engineered, and maintained by DnK Labs ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy, security, and personal data of our users ("you" or "learner").

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how it is handled and secured, and your rights regarding your personal data and account deletion under Google Play Developer Policies and global privacy regulations.

2. Information We Collect & Why

To provide a seamless, synchronized, and engaging developer learning experience, RN Master Class collects and syncs the following information to our secure servers:

  • Account Credentials: Name, email address, and encrypted/hashed password when you sign up or log in.
  • Profile Information: User title (e.g. Junior/Senior Developer), profile photo (avatar or uploaded picture), and avatar styling preferences.
  • Learning Progress & Streaks: Completed component topics out of the 47+ curriculum lessons, daily streak counts, and last active learning dates to maintain your educational continuity across devices.
  • Quiz & Practical Scores: Interactive quiz assessments, practical test results, score history, and certification progress.
  • App Usage & Session Analytics: Cumulative active learning time and session milestones, allowing you to monitor your study habits on your profile.
  • Support & Feedback Messages: Questions, feedback, or support queries sent through in-app channels.

💡 Offline Preferences: Visual theme (Light/Dark mode), app language selection (English, Gujarati, Hindi), and audio/haptic feedback settings are saved locally on your device via AsyncStorage.

3. Device Permissions & Usage

Our application requests only the minimal device permissions strictly required to deliver interactive learning features:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used exclusively when you choose to capture a new profile photo directly within the profile editor. The camera is never accessed in the background or without your explicit action.
  • Android Photo Picker (No Broad Storage Permission): When choosing a profile picture from your device, RN Master Class uses the modern, privacy-friendly Android Photo Picker. We do not request READ_MEDIA_IMAGES or full gallery access; only the single photo you explicitly pick is shared with the app.
  • Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to schedule optional daily streak study reminders at 7:00 PM to keep your learning momentum alive. You can enable or disable notifications at any time from the in-app Settings screen.

4. Data Storage, Security & Encryption

All communications between the RN Master Class mobile application and our backend servers are strictly encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS (TLS) encryption. Passwords and sensitive authentication tokens are hashed before storage.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or advertising networks.

5. User Account & Data Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

In accordance with Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion Policy, learners have full control to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated data at any time.

⚠️ What gets deleted when you delete your account:

  • Your user account, name, email address, and encrypted credentials.
  • Your uploaded profile photo and avatar data.
  • Your completed curriculum lessons, badges, streaks, and quiz histories.
  • Your tracked app usage time and support messages.

You can request account deletion using either of the following two methods:

A
In-App Deletion (Immediate)

Open the RN Master Class app → Tap Settings → Scroll down to DATA & STORAGE → Tap "Delete Account & Reset Data" → Confirm deletion. The server immediately purges your account and resets your local data.

B
Web / Email Deletion Request (Without App Installed)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, you can request permanent account and data deletion by sending an email from your registered email address to dnklabs07@gmail.com.

🗑️ Request Account Deletion via Email

Processing timeline: Email deletion requests are verified and permanently wiped from our production database within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application and learning curriculum are intended for developers and students aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements in our app architecture or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted directly to this web page with a refreshed effective date.
